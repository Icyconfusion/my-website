DD sim wheel

The DD sim wheel is a project I designed for Hackclub Stasis YSWS. It is designed to replicate the feel of commercial sim racing wheelbases, which can deliver high torques. This creates an extremely realistic feel, as if you are driving a real racing car. Sim racing is a passion of mine, so it was a real joy to design this, and I learned a lot designing my own motor driver.
